01 Research

Safer ground, sooner.

Landmines and explosive remnants of war keep killing and injuring people long after the fighting stops, and they keep farmland, roads and villages out of use. Clearing that land is slow, careful and dangerous work.

My PhD in the Robotics and Autonomous Systems group at the University of Oslo’s Department of Technology Systems explores how robotics can support that work: drones and autonomous systems that help survey and clearance teams decide where to look, see more, and spend less time in harm’s way.

Interest · A

Swarm autonomy: coordinating multiple robots to cover ground together.
