Research

Landmines and other explosive ordnance stay dangerous long after a conflict ends. My PhD looks at how drones and other robots can help survey teams find them, so the land can be cleared and used again.

Robotics and Autonomous Systems group, Department of Technology Systems, University of Oslo. Since November 2025.

Interests

  • Aerial survey with drones
  • Perception in real field conditions
  • Swarm autonomy
  • Tools that fit how mine action teams work
